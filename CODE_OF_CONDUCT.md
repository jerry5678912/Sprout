# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Sprout should be a welcoming place to learn, build, question decisions, and
contribute. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with patience and respect;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- make room for newcomers and different experience levels;
- accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and
  publication of another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code
review, community spaces, and public interactions made on behalf of Sprout.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-advisory
contact:

<https://github.com/jerry5678912/Sprout/security/advisories/new>

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily/permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for the current
size of the Sprout project.
